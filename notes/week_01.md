1] --12.10.2026-- 1.2 - Sample spaces and Pebble World

Sample space S of an experiment is the set of all possible outcomes. An event A is a part of sample S, and we define that A happens, if the outcome is equal to A.
The sample space can be finite, countably infinite, uncountably infinite. When its finite, we can visualize it as Pebble World. 
Each pebble is an outcome, and stack of pebbles are event.

A u B -> A or B
A n B -> A and B 
A^(c) -> Not A
